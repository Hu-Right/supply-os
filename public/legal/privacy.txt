政策摘要

我们遵循合法、正当、必要、诚信和最小必要原则处理个人信息，并向用户提供查阅、复制、更正、补充、删除、撤回同意、注销账号等权利行使渠道。

We process personal information lawfully, fairly, in good faith and on a data-minimisation basis, and provide mechanisms for access, copy, correction, supplementation, deletion, withdrawal of consent and account closure.

OS NEO SMART 属于面向企业国际公共采购与出海业务的服务平台。企业信息中如包含自然人姓名、工作邮箱、电话、职位等，也可能属于个人信息。

OS NEO SMART is a business platform for international public procurement and overseas-business support. Enterprise data may also constitute personal information where it contains identifiable individuals, such as names, work emails, phone numbers or job titles.

1. 我们是谁及本政策适用范围

本政策适用于杭州云境智展科技有限公司运营的 OS NEO SMART 网站（osneosmart.com）以及明确引用本政策的相关网页、账户系统、会员服务和在线功能。

本政策解释我们如何收集、存储、使用、加工、传输、提供、公开和删除个人信息。与某项线下项目、代投标、培训或专项企业服务另行签署的数据处理约定，与本政策共同适用。

This Policy applies to the OS NEO SMART website (osneosmart.com) operated by 杭州云境智展科技有限公司, and to related pages, accounts, memberships and online functions that expressly reference this Policy.

It explains how we collect, store, use, process, transmit, provide, disclose and delete personal information. Data-processing terms in a separate offline project, bid-support, training or enterprise-service agreement apply together with this Policy.

2. 我们处理个人信息的基本原则

我们按照明确、合理的目的处理个人信息，并仅处理与目的直接相关的最小范围信息；不通过误导、欺诈或胁迫方式取得同意。

基于同意处理时，您的同意应在充分知情前提下自愿、明确作出。法律要求单独同意或书面同意的，我们将按要求取得。

对于提供核心服务非必要的信息，您有权拒绝提供；除法律另有规定或该信息确属提供相应功能所必需外，我们不会仅因您不同意额外处理而拒绝提供基本服务。

We process personal information for specific and reasonable purposes and limit processing to what is directly relevant and necessary. Consent is not obtained through deception, fraud or coercion.

Where processing is based on consent, consent must be voluntary, informed and explicit. Separate or written consent will be obtained where required by law.

You may refuse to provide information that is not necessary for the core service. Except where required by law or necessary for the requested feature, we will not deny basic services merely because you decline optional processing.

我们根据您实际使用的功能收集不同信息。以下为本平台典型场景的信息收集说明。

The information we collect depends on the functions you use. The descriptions below explain typical data-collection scenarios.

3.2 敏感个人信息

平台原则上不以收集敏感个人信息作为普通注册的必要条件。若企业认证、退款、合同签署、特定投标项目确需处理身份证件、金融账户、精确位置、特定身份等敏感个人信息，我们将说明必要性、对个人权益的影响，并在法律要求时取得单独同意。

As a rule, sensitive personal information is not required for ordinary registration. If enterprise verification, refunds, contracting or a specific tender project genuinely requires identity documents, financial-account information, precise location, specific identity information or other sensitive data, we will explain necessity and impact and obtain separate consent where required by law.

4. 我们如何使用个人信息

我们主要将个人信息用于以下目的：创建和管理账号；识别和验证企业；提供采购搜索、匹配、报告与会员权益；完成订单、开票和售后；响应咨询与投诉；防止欺诈、攻击和滥用；改进产品与服务；履行法律法规要求。

如拟将个人信息用于与原处理目的无直接关联的新目的，我们会依法重新告知并在需要时重新取得同意。

We primarily use personal information to create and manage accounts; identify and verify enterprises; provide procurement search, matching, reports and membership benefits; fulfil orders, invoicing and after-sales support; respond to requests and complaints; prevent fraud, attacks and abuse; improve products and services; and comply with law.

If we intend to use personal information for a new purpose not directly related to the original purpose, we will provide renewed notice and obtain renewed consent where required.

5. 采购公开信息与职业联系信息

平台可能从合法公开来源获得采购公告、采购机构联系人、企业联系人、工作邮箱、工作电话、职位等信息，并用于采购信息索引、来源回溯、机构识别、项目分析和企业服务。

公开可访问并不意味着个人信息可以无限制使用。对于可识别自然人的公开信息，我们将结合信息公开目的、合理预期、使用方式及对个人权益影响进行处理；如处理对个人权益有重大影响，依法取得同意或满足其他合法处理条件。

如您认为平台展示的职业联系信息不准确、超出合理范围或应删除，可通过隐私权利渠道提交申请。

The Platform may obtain procurement notices, procuring-entity contacts, enterprise contacts, work emails, work phone numbers and job titles from lawful public sources and use them for indexing, source traceability, institutional identification, project analysis and enterprise services.

Public availability does not mean personal information may be used without limitation. For identifiable public information, we consider the purpose for which it was made public, reasonable expectations, manner of use and impact on the individual. Where processing materially affects individual rights, we will obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis as required.

If you believe professional contact information displayed by the Platform is inaccurate, excessive or should be removed, you may submit a request through our privacy-rights channel.

为维持登录状态、账号安全、语言与界面偏好和基础功能，我们可能使用必要Cookie或本地存储。

对于统计分析、个性化推荐、广告或其他非必要Cookie与类似技术，平台根据适用法律和实际部署提供相应告知与选择机制；在用户作出选择前，不启动需要同意的非必要追踪。

Cookie名称、提供方、目的、有效期和关闭方式可在Cookie设置或相关清单中查看。

We may use strictly necessary cookies or local storage to maintain sessions, account security, language and interface preferences and core functions.

For analytics, personalised recommendations, advertising or other non-essential cookies and similar technologies, the Platform provides notice and choices appropriate to applicable law and actual deployment. Tracking requiring consent will not start before the user has made the required choice.

Cookie names, providers, purposes, durations and control methods are available in cookie settings or a related list.

为提供服务，我们可能委托云计算、短信与邮件、验证码、支付、客服、日志监控、数据分析、AI与API等供应商处理必要信息。我们将根据适用法律对受托方进行评估，并通过合同约定处理目的、期限、方式、信息种类和保护措施。

如我们向其他独立个人信息处理者提供您的个人信息，我们将依法向您告知接收方信息、处理目的与方式和个人信息种类，并在需要时取得单独同意、开展个人信息保护影响评估。

To provide services, we may engage cloud, SMS and email, verification, payment, customer support, monitoring, analytics, AI and API vendors to process necessary information. We will assess processors as required and contractually define purpose, duration, method, data categories and safeguards.

If we provide your personal information to another independent personal-information processor, we will provide legally required information about the recipient, purpose and method and data categories, obtain separate consent where required and conduct a personal-information protection impact assessment where applicable.

The Platform publishes a Third-Party Information Sharing List identifying each real third party, purpose, data involved, processing method, privacy-policy link and potential cross-border element.

8. 个人信息跨境提供

OS NEO SMART 具有国际业务属性，但国际业务本身不等于用户个人信息必然出境。我们将优先根据业务必要性控制跨境传输范围。

如因境外云服务、国际采购平台接口、境外合作方、跨境客服、AI与API服务或履行用户主动发起的跨境业务确需向境外提供个人信息，我们将依据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》《促进和规范数据跨境流动规定》等适用规则，判断是否属于豁免情形，或履行安全评估、个人信息出境标准合同或备案、个人信息保护认证等适用程序，并依法进行告知、取得单独同意和开展影响评估。

跨境接收方、国家或地区、数据类别、目的和机制以实际数据流为准，并在适用时向用户提供明确说明。

OS NEO SMART operates internationally, but international business does not itself mean that user personal information is necessarily transferred abroad. We seek to minimise cross-border data flows based on business necessity.

Where personal information must be provided abroad due to overseas cloud services, procurement-platform integrations, overseas partners, cross-border support, AI and API services or a user-initiated cross-border transaction, we will assess exemptions and, where applicable, complete security assessment, standard-contract or filing, certification or other mechanisms under the PIPL, Provisions on Facilitating and Regulating Cross-Border Data Flows and other applicable rules. Notice, separate consent and impact assessment will be completed where required.

Recipients, countries or regions, data categories, purposes and legal mechanisms will be based on actual data flows and disclosed where applicable.

9. 存储地点与保存期限

原则上，我们将在中华人民共和国境内存储在境内运营中收集和产生的个人信息；若实际使用境外存储或境外可远程访问，将按照"个人信息跨境提供"章节执行并据实更新本政策。

个人信息保存期限以实现处理目的所必要的最短时间为原则。账号信息通常保存至账号注销或服务终止后依法必要的期限；订单、合同、发票、财务和交易记录按照适用法律规定保存；安全日志按网络安全、审计和风险管理所需的合理期限保存。

达到保存期限后，我们将删除或匿名化个人信息；因法律规定不能立即删除时，将停止除存储和必要安全保护之外的处理。

As a general rule, personal information collected and generated through operations in Mainland China will be stored in Mainland China. If overseas storage or overseas remote access is in fact used, the Cross-Border Transfers section will apply and this Policy must be updated to reflect reality.

Retention is limited to the shortest period necessary for the processing purpose. Account data is generally retained until account closure or service termination plus any legally required period; order, contract, invoice, finance and transaction records are retained as required by law; security logs are retained for a reasonable period needed for cybersecurity, audit and risk management.

After the applicable period, data is deleted or anonymised. Where immediate deletion is prohibited or technically infeasible due to legal retention requirements, processing will be limited to storage and necessary protection.

10. 信息安全措施

我们采取与业务和风险相适应的访问控制、身份认证、权限最小化、日志审计、备份、漏洞修复、传输保护、必要的加密或去标识化等管理和技术措施。

企业投标材料、认证材料等高敏感业务文件实行更严格的访问权限和最小可见原则。内部员工和外部受托方仅在职责和授权范围内访问。

如发生个人信息安全事件，我们将依法采取补救措施，并在法律要求时向主管部门报告、向受影响个人告知。

We use organisational and technical safeguards appropriate to business and risk, including access control, authentication, least-privilege permissions, logging and audit, backups, vulnerability remediation, transmission protection and, where appropriate, encryption or de-identification.

Enterprise tender and verification materials are subject to stricter access controls and least-visibility principles. Employees and processors may access them only within authorised duties.

In the event of a personal-information security incident, we will take remedial measures and, where required, report to authorities and notify affected individuals.

11. 自动化决策与个性化推荐

平台可能根据您输入的产品、行业、UNSPSC、搜索与收藏行为等进行采购机会推荐或匹配。此类推荐主要用于提升信息发现效率。

如自动化决策对个人权益产生重大影响，我们将依法提高透明度并提供相应说明、拒绝或其他救济机制。用于商业营销或信息推送时，提供非针对个人特征的选项或便捷拒绝方式。

The Platform may recommend or match procurement opportunities based on product, industry, UNSPSC, search and favourite behaviour and similar data to improve information discovery.

Where automated decision-making materially affects individual rights, we will provide legally required transparency and appropriate explanation, opt-out or other safeguards. For marketing or information pushes, we will provide a non-personalised option or an accessible method to refuse personalised targeting as required.

12. 您的个人信息权利

在适用法律规定范围内，您有权查阅、复制、更正、补充、删除、限制处理、撤回同意、注销账号，并在符合法定条件时请求转移个人信息。

平台在个人中心提供便捷入口，或通过"隐私权利申请"与"联系我们"渠道受理。我们可能为保护账号安全核验申请人身份，但不得设置与请求无关的不合理条件。

撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意开展的处理活动的效力。对于提供服务所必需且具有其他合法处理基础的信息，即使撤回可选授权，我们仍可能在必要范围内继续处理。

Subject to applicable law, you may access, copy, correct, supplement, delete, restrict processing, withdraw consent, close your account and, where statutory conditions are met, request transfer of your personal information.

The Platform provides an accessible account-centre entry or processes requests through a Privacy Rights or Contact Us channel. We may verify identity for security but will not impose unreasonable conditions unrelated to the request.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing lawfully conducted before withdrawal. If certain information remains necessary for a service and another lawful basis applies, limited processing may continue as permitted by law.

13. 账号注销

用户申请注销后，我们将核验身份并处理未完成订单、退款、争议和必要的安全事项。完成注销后，除法律法规要求保留的信息外，我们将删除或匿名化与账号直接关联的个人信息。

注销入口不设置不合理障碍，不要求必须线下到场或提供与注册无关的额外材料。详细的注销规则请参见《OS NEO SMART 账号注销与个人信息管理规则》。

After a closure request, we verify identity and resolve outstanding orders, refunds, disputes and necessary security matters. Once closure is complete, personal information directly associated with the account will be deleted or anonymised except where retention is legally required.

The closure process does not impose unreasonable barriers such as in-person attendance or documents unrelated to account registration. For details, please refer to the OS NEO SMART Account Cancellation and Personal Information Management Rules.

14. 未成年人保护

OS NEO SMART 主要面向具有企业业务需求的成年人和组织用户，不以不满十四周岁的未成年人为目标用户。

如我们发现无适当监护人同意而处理了不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息，将依法尽快删除或采取其他必要措施。如未来专门向未成年人提供服务，将制定专门个人信息处理规则。

OS NEO SMART is primarily intended for adults and organisations with business needs and is not directed at children under 14.

If we learn that we processed personal information of a child under 14 without appropriate guardian consent, we will delete it or take other necessary measures as required by law. If services are later specifically offered to minors, a dedicated child privacy rule will be adopted.

如处理目的、方式、信息种类等发生变化且依法需要重新取得同意，我们将在新处理开始前重新取得同意。历史版本保留以供查询。

We may update this Policy due to changes in law, regulation, data processing or product functions. Material changes will be highlighted through pop-ups, notices, in-product messages or other prominent means.

Where processing purpose, method or data category changes and renewed consent is legally required, we will obtain it before the new processing begins. Historical versions are retained for reference.
